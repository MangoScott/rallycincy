How it works

Join the next one →
1

Show up

No sign-ups, no fees, no partner required. Bring a racket and whatever shoes you have. We meet at the courts a few minutes before 7:30 and say hi.

2

Rally & rotate

We split into groups by level and hit. Every 15 minutes or so we rotate, so you play with everyone. Points if you want them, rallies if you don't.

3

Hang after

The rally is the excuse. Afterward we grab a drink or a bite nearby. That part is optional, but it's where the friends get made.

Good to know

Questions people ask

I haven't played in years. Is that okay?

Yes. Rusty is the default. We group by level so you'll be hitting with people who are also finding their swing. Nobody keeps score unless both of you want to.

Do I need a partner?

No. Most people come alone the first time. We rotate through the night, so you'll hit with a bunch of different people before it's over.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The courts are public, the balls are covered, and the company is free. If you want to chip in for balls down the line, we'll never say no, but it's never expected.

What if it rains?

We call it by 5 PM on Instagram. Light drizzle we usually play through. Wet courts we don't, because sliding into the net is not the vibe.

I don't have a racket.

Come anyway. We keep a few loaners in the bag. Message ahead if you can so we make sure one is there for you.

Can I bring a friend?

Please do. The whole thing runs on people bringing people.
